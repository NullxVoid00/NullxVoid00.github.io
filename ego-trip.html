VRChat Roguelite Journey Through the Red Shard

Touch the crystal to watch the opening.

Within the crimson crystal lies a space between consciousness and dream. As Null awakens alongside shadowy echo-fragments, they must venture through an endless labyrinth where lost souls have built their own twisted domains. Face the corrupted, collect Remnants and discover what waits beyond Mender’s Haven.

WHAT IS EGO/TRIP?

One more room. One more chance.

Choose an echo, explore a shifting labyrinth and build around the Remnants you find. Venture alone or with up to three friends. Each run brings new encounters, discoveries and a choice about when to bring your finds back to Mender’s Haven.

🎮
Desktop & VR

Solo or up to four players in VRChat. PC and standalone Quest builds are in development, with controls tailored to each platform.

🌀
A changing labyrinth

Combat, shops, rests, memories and optional activities break up the journey. Character-specific side paths reward curiosity without blocking the main route.

⚔️
Find your fighting style

Knives, impact punches, a shotgun bat or a grappling scythe. Guard, parry, move and build around what feels right to you.

📈
Ten evolving levels

Weapons gain new capabilities as you earn EXP. Damage can cost EXP and levels, so staying strong means staying sharp.

💎
Build with Remnants

Equip discoveries that change how your actions work. Mix effects, store finds in the Haven and share physical loot with your party.

🍺
A Haven worth returning to

Furnish Mender’s bar, meet its patrons, collect library entries, take on arena cups and unlock new rooms.

PLAYABLE ALPHA · ~70% OVERALL PROGRESS

DOMAINS & ENCOUNTERS

Encounter previews, original artwork and real development captures. Each domain has its own atmosphere and challenges.

THE SERPENT

Ink, accusation & dangerous heights

Dusty archives and broken text give way to an ascent along a colossal spine. Break the knots, reach the head, then prepare for a more dangerous climb. Read the obstacles instead of rushing every opening.

THE QUEEN

A garden with a crown of thorns

Courtyards, banquet spaces and towers lead to a giant whose arms become your route upward. Strike the attached head first; later, the detached head becomes a threat while the heart becomes your goal.

THE CHARADE

Behind the performance

Theater-like rooms and shifting moods lead into an immense hall. Follow the retreating figure, respond to the current mask and use the routes created by the encounter. Anger, sadness and happiness ask different things of you.

THE MOON

A beautiful, lonely night

Wistful playrooms open onto an endless ocean. Stand on the water, watch its surface and read the hands, waves and sweeping light. When an opening appears, the arena is still dangerous.

THE RED DEPTHS

The last stretch of the labyrinth

Crimson geometry, black obelisks and signs of a desperate climb. Tougher enemy combinations, traps and vertical routes ask you to use everything you have learned. The final boss is still in development.

GAMEPLAY SYSTEMS

⚔️
Guard. Parry. Commit.

Guard to soften a hit; time a parry to create an opening. Enemy support roles and elite variants make target choice matter.

📊
Level 1 → 10

Earn EXP through combat. Higher levels require more EXP and expand your base weapon, while taking damage can bring you back down.

🎒
Bring it home

Satchel space is limited. Trade physical finds with friends, decide what to carry and bank them in the Haven.

💎
Four slots to start

Each character starts with four Remnant compartments. Four main boss rewards expand that loadout; a final postgame modifier slot is planned.

🗺️
Follow the party

The leader chooses the next room. The map tracks explored rooms and party locations; loading transitions show your progress toward the next encounter.

✦
Bring a friend back

A fallen teammate leaves a colored revive light. Reviving takes time and some of the rescuer’s health. Dead players can watch the party.

Rest before you leave

Rest checkpoints let you return to an in-progress run. Use the proper Haven door to bank discoveries; abandoning a run can lose unbanked items. The host owns the shared Haven save.

Found notes, encountered enemies and discovered Remnants become part of your library. The in-world book presents them with pop-up scenes and miniature models.

MENDER’S HAVEN

Make room for yourself

Choose an echo, organize your Remnants and turn the bar into a place you want to return to. Furniture fits floors, walls, ceilings and tabletops; paint finishes become reusable after banking.

🛍️
Discover → bank → buy

Bring furnishings back from the labyrinth to add them to Mender’s stock. Ordinary pieces can be purchased repeatedly. Room doors are one-time unlocks.

🍺
Attract new company

Seating and specific decorations attract patrons. NPCs choose places around your furniture; some have stories or quests. Defeated bosses can join after you collect their notes.

📖
Build a collection

Place a library door to browse found notes, enemy details and Remnants. The book is part of a room you can visit, not a wall of tiny text.

🏆
Take another challenge

Arena cups offer themed gauntlets and exclusive rewards. An achievement room celebrates meaningful milestones, with more to unlock as you progress.

THE ARENA

Choose a cup, face its encounters and earn its unique reward. The crowd reacts to the fight. New cups and achievement-gated challenges give strong builds another place to shine.

Something different between fights

Optional Event Rooms include fishing, timed races, a Break Room full of things to smash and miniboss encounters. In co-op, some activities add friendly competition.
