Keep moving. Keep building.

Learn a character, make a build, and survive long enough to see what it becomes.

01

Make space to survive

Move through waves, collect experience and choose upgrades. Enemy approaches, rush packs, stage hazards and bosses put pressure on your route.

02

Chase an evolution

Build a weapon to level eight, pair it with the right passive and open an eligible chest. Evolutions and weapon unions turn a familiar attack into a different kind of build.

03

Bring back progress

Stage clears, discoveries and achievements unlock more characters, locations and options. Collect maps, consult the bestiary and return with permanent upgrades.

Beyond a single arena

Different places, different reasons to explore.

Original journeys

Start in The Forsaken Courtyard, then push toward places such as Moonfall Hamlet, Frostwatch Crossing, Castle Blackglass and Kino Casino. Later unlocks include Absentia’s Crimson Graveyard, Amygdala’s Silent Core and a boss rush.

Selectable stages range from 15 to 30 minutes. Map discoveries, special encounters and character conditions give a run goals beyond simply waiting out the clock.

Fan crossover destinations

The roster also visits worlds inspired by Undertale, Deltarune, Cave Story, Ib, Yume Nikki and Mad Father. They bring distinct stage layouts, enemies and unlockable characters.

These are fan crossover elements in Wraith Blade, with the original games and characters credited to their creators.

A build of your own. A fight together.

Team play shares the pressure without turning every player into the same character.

01

Up to four players

The project includes a co-op lobby, player-specific views, shared encounters, chest choices and revives.

02

Discover the roster

Characters can be earned through stage conditions and discoveries. New playable additions include Null, Neuro and crossover characters with their own starting weapons.

03

Keep something between runs

Permanent upgrades, a bestiary, achievements and a music archive extend progression. Clearing stages opens more destinations and their music.

Development

Far beyond the first arena.

Large playable foundation · active development

The current content inventory contains 19 selectable stages plus a special trial, 19 characters, 55 weapon definitions, 28 evolution recipes and 82 achievements. Those are content counts, not a promise that balance and every multiplayer situation are finished.

In development · substantial playable content
Recent work
Expanded stages and roster, equipment evolutions, enemy variety, co-op, permanent upgrades, chest presentation and a music archive.
Still ahead
Further balance and presentation work. Planned card-style run modifiers and NPC companions are not implemented.
Testing continues
Live multiplayer, headset performance and player comfort still need ongoing real-world playtests.
