Fast movement. Deliberate choices.

Build a plan without turning every fight into a shooting gallery.

01

Move, then commit

Move in real time while the action gauge fills. Open the action menu to plan; committing an attack resumes the fight while Qubit carries it out.

02

Read the threat

Cyberhounds lunge, Watchdrones strafe and lock their aim, and heavier security units pressure your position. Walls and firing lanes matter.

03

Choose the trade-off

Piercing Barrel, Fan Array and Siege Coil alter how you fire. Rage and Haste fragments give useful power with costs, from damage vulnerability to slower reloads.

Equipment uses a grid inventory. You can carry spare attachments, but only one barrel is equipped at a time. Extraction turns recovered gear into future loadout options.
Somewhere to return to

A hub with more than a launch button.

Five connected districts

The Concourse, Patch Market, Memory Gallery, Uplink and Echo’s Refuge form the current safehouse. Workshop, repair, recovery, dialogue and archive stations give recovered items a home.

Progress that survives a run

Banked credits, recovered blueprints and your chosen next-run loadout persist. Continue returns to the safehouse; it does not resume halfway through an expedition.
