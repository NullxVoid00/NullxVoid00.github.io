🌟 VRChat Remake Features

🏃‍♂️ Adventure Movement
Speed, momentum, spin dashes and character movement adapted for VRChat. Loops, kart sections and stage-specific traversal continue to receive playability and tuning passes.
👥 Six Familiar Characters
Sonic, Tails, Knuckles, Amy, Big and Gamma each have a character foundation. Sonic has the most developed story route; the other campaigns are not yet complete.
🎬 A Connected Sonic Story
Adventure Fields, cutscenes, action stages and boss encounters now join into Sonic’s development route through the finale and credits. Short cards stand in for some unfinished story transitions.
🗺️ Adventure Fields
Travel through Station Square, Mystic Ruins and the Egg Carrier between the action. Hub connections, arrivals and transport routes are part of the connected journey.
🎮 Desktop & VR
Explore with desktop controls or a headset. The adaptation preserves familiar characters and scenery while its interaction and movement systems are tuned for VRChat.
⏱️ Return to the Action
Trial stages let you revisit action stages without advancing the story. Practice a section, explore another route or return to a favorite setting.
🛠️ In Development
Other campaigns, exact boss choreography, transport animations and special-stage polish remain in progress. The published world may be behind the development version described here.
🎵 Familiar Sounds
Sonic Adventure music and sound effects accompany the project. The page’s original music and sound sliders remain available after pressing Start.
