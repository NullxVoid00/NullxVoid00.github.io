Think fast. Take your turn.

  • Rolling HP: damage counts down instead of immediately disappearing from the meter.
  • Party commands: choose actions and targets, use PSI and manage your items.
  • Surrounding battle scenes: psychedelic backdrops bring the encounter around you, with gentler motion options.
  • Readable results: step through battle messages at your own pace.

A world between the fights.

  • Goods and equipment: pack what you need, choose equipment and make room for discoveries.
  • Town services: shops, hospitals, hotels, phones and ATMs support the journey.
  • Three save slots: record your own progress through the adventure.
  • A useful map: check your location and an optional objective cue while exploring.

Good to know

Your adventure, your pace.

Is this the entire EarthBound game?

Not yet. This is an early fan remake, with the opening through Twoson and arrival in Threed as the current playable development scope. Later chapters are not being presented as complete.

Do visitors share a battle party?

No. Each visitor has an independent adventure and save progress. You can be in the same VRChat world without sharing story flags, inventory or a battle party.

How do I save or recover?

Use a telephone and call Dad to record the current adventure in your active save slot. Mom can restore your HP and PP; hotels and hospitals provide other recovery options as you travel. Saving and continuing become available after VRChat finishes restoring your data.

Do I need VR?

The project includes desktop movement and interaction as well as VR pointing and selection. The battle background can be set to a still or gentler presentation.

Is there a public release?

This page is a development showcase. A public play link will be added when there is a release ready to share.
