Take your seat

A familiar ritual, in a virtual room.

Games have something you can hold, open and share before you even start playing.

01

A shelf worth browsing

Pick up a game box, inspect its artwork, open the booklet and insert the removable disc. Each game has its own package and identity.

02

Play together, or watch

Pick up a free controller and connect when you are ready. Multiplayer support depends on the game; spectators can follow the shared display without taking a seat.

03

Make yourself comfortable

The console includes TV and handheld presentation, controller help, game-specific saves and a media browser. Return Home to manage the console without losing the idea of a shared living room.

First session

From shelf to screen.

Power on

Start the console and browse its home screen.

Insert a game

Open a box and place its disc into the console.

Connect a controller

Pick up an available controller, then press Jump to connect. On desktop, that is Space.

Follow the game

Use the game’s title screen and booklet. Some games share a party; others have one pilot and an audience.

How many people can play?

Cave Story and Madness Retaliation support up to four controller participants in their implemented co-op modes. Qubit currently has one pilot, with others watching. A game’s player count is listed on its own page.

Between adventures

The console is also a place to hang out.

Media features include personal playlists and a shared playback queue. Video availability depends on the source and VRChat’s playback support.

Development

A working console, still growing.

Playable framework · active development

The physical-game workflow, game modules, console home, controllers and media features are implemented. Work now includes expanding the library, refining comfort and checking how each game behaves with real players.
