Two ways to retaliate

The campaign, or a new route.

Original campaign

Room by room. Fight by fight.

Explore the original room network, find keycards and equipment, manage the squad’s supplies and take on its major encounters. The conversion includes the original title, equipment interface, music and story sequences.

Added mode

Irregularity

Choose a seed and Standard or Veteran difficulty, then navigate a branching 27-node route across three sectors. Combat, supply stops, elites and bosses lead to new loadout decisions.

Salvage gear into shared stock and equip at safe nodes. The new run mode has its own checkpoint, separate from campaign saves.

One squad. Shared decisions.

Co-op splits responsibility, not the adventure.

01

Control your soldiers

One player commands all four soldiers. With two players, each gets two; three players split 2/1/1; four players each command one.

02

Commit together

Move, attack and manage action points with your assigned units. Ready up when finished; the enemy turn waits for the eligible squad and queued actions.

03

Keep the team supplied

Equipment, spare gear and consumables belong to the shared squad. The leader handles travel and shared run choices, while player colors make assignments readable.

Before your first fight

Connect, choose, and commit.

Connect

Insert the disc, pick up a controller, then press Jump / Space. Holding the controller alone does not start control.

Choose the mode

Start or load the campaign, or select Irregularity. Turn Co-op on before starting if friends are joining.

Plan a turn

Select your soldier, choose an action and its target, then confirm. Watch the action-point cost.

Save between fights

Use Squad Commands → Save / Load in the campaign. Irregularity has a separate cleared-node checkpoint.

Saving is explicit, not automatic. In co-op the leader operates save/load, and joining a squad does not overwrite your own personal save.
