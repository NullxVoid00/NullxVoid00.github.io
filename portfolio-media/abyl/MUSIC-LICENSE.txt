ABYL ORIGINAL ADVENTURE MUSIC
Created for this project, 2026-10-31.

Workshop - 96 BPM, D major, 16 bars, 40.000 seconds.
Town (Lantern Vale) - 112 BPM, G major, 16 bars, 34.286 seconds.
Overworld (Lantern Coast) - 124 BPM, E Dorian, 16 bars, 30.968 seconds.
Battle - 144 BPM, A minor, 16 bars, 26.667 seconds.

These four original compositions were synthesized from authored melodies, diatonic chord progressions, bass parts, arpeggios and percussion. No third-party audio, samples, soundfonts, recordings or transcribed melodies were used. The rendering engine is preserved in outputs/music-source/AbylComposer.cs. Each track is a complete 16-bar phrase. Release tails and stereo delays wrap across the end into the beginning for continuous playback. Files are stereo PCM16 at 22050 Hz; Unity imports them as streaming Vorbis for a smaller world build.
