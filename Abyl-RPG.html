SYSTEM OVERVIEW

Remember the magic of your first RPG? Exploring a little town, preparing for the next encounter, and watching your party grow? Abyl brings that kind of adventure into VRChat—and is being built to help you create your own.

The project now includes a connected party, turn-based battles, an adventure journal, towns, an overworld, quests, and a growing set of guided Unity editors. It is a working development framework, with more testing and polish ahead.

For Players:

  • Explore towns and miniature landscapes, talk to residents, and follow quests.
  • Choose battle actions, manage equipment and abilities, and earn experience and gold.
  • Prepare at shops and inns before heading back into danger.

For Creators:

Begin with a clean adventure or the Creator Garden quest sample. Build maps, write conversations, connect rewards and gates, and arrange encounters through the guided tools.

COMBAT MECHANICS

Every Turn Is a Decision:

Attack, defend, spend MP on a skill, use an item, or try to flee. Pick the right target and decide when the party needs damage, protection, or recovery.

  • Visible encounters: Enemies face the party in a dedicated battle arena.
  • Abilities and effects: Mix damage, healing, and supported status effects.
  • Battle rewards: Experience and gold carry the adventure forward.

From Exploration to Battle:

A frozen-view crack and shatter transition introduces encounters while keeping head movement free in VR. A comfort fade is available as an alternative. Area music gives way to the battle track and returns after the encounter.

CHARACTER PROGRESSION

Your Character, Your Build:

The adventure journal gathers party details, character growth, inventory, and skills in one place. Gain experience, spend growth points, and prepare your equipment and supplies for the next encounter.

  • Equipment: The item and equipment tools support nine equipment slots.
  • Supplies: Consumables, starting items, and shop stock are editable by the creator.
  • Shops and inns: Buy or sell eligible items and pay for rest when the adventure offers it.

Current Checkpoint Support:

Creators can opt into host checkpoints. These preserve shared adventure progress and the host's character, inventory, equipment, and reward claims. Resuming currently requires a fresh solo instance; other party members' characters are not saved by that host checkpoint.

MULTIPLAYER FEATURES

Adventures Are Better With Friends:

Abyl is designed around a party journey. The current starter provides four character stations, shared encounters, connected travel, and party information in the journal.

Shared Adventure, Clear Rewards:

Creators can make an interaction repeatable, once for the party, or once for each participant. Quests, gates, and changes to the world are part of the shared adventure state.

Still in Development:

Multiplayer support is implemented, but live VRChat testing is still needed for late joins, players leaving, host changes, and other edge cases. Desktop and headset controls, comfort, and Quest performance also need hands-on acceptance. This is not a finished-release guarantee.

MAKE IT YOUR GAME

Start From a Working Example:

The New Adventure workflow creates either a clean starter or a small quest sample. Creator Garden includes Rowan's parcel quest, a second sanctuary quest, a key chest, a gate, a shop and paid inn, a cutscene, a practice battle, and a checkpoint.

Guided Creator Tools:

  • World building: Paint tiles and height, create rooms, roads, bridges and ramps, and import rectangular tilesheets.
  • Characters: Import directional sprites and write portrait-based dialogue choices.
  • Quests: Connect conditions, independent quest stages, items, gold, travel, rewards and scenery changes.
  • Presentation: Set up music zones, sound cues, cutscenes and interface themes.

Validation tools help check a created adventure. More creator onboarding and release preparation remain ahead.

ENEMIES & AI

Create Your Own Bestiary:

Enemy templates define the opponents in your encounters. The creator tools let you arrange formations and select supported abilities, while the combat system handles their turns, targets, damage and effects.

Build Encounters With a Purpose:

  • Choose enemy combinations for a formation.
  • Use danger regions for exploration encounters and keep towns safe.
  • Connect a battle to an interaction, quest step, or story moment.
  • Adjust enemy stats and abilities to match the adventure's intended difficulty.

The current sample demonstrates the foundation. A larger, polished bestiary and complete adventure content still depend on the worlds made with it.

WORLD & NPCS

Places With Something to Do:

Lantern Vale is the current prototype town: open buildings, a fountain square, shops, an inn, residents, branching conversation, and a delivery quest. The miniature Lantern Coast links paths, forests, mountains, a bridge, ruins, and a cave.

Make the World React:

  • Write conversations with choices and portraits.
  • Run more than one quest at a time.
  • Open a gate, reveal scenery, give a reward, or start a cutscene from an interaction.
  • Connect destinations with linked travel points and music changes.

The overworld's smaller scenery creates a classic RPG sense of scale; it does not resize the player's avatar.

TARGETING SYSTEM

Choose an Action, Then Its Target:

Battle commands lead into target selection for the appropriate opponent, ally, or self. The goal is to keep the decision readable while you remain present in the arena.

  • Offense: Direct damage and supported harmful effects toward enemies.
  • Support: Choose an ally or yourself for healing and buffs.
  • Information: Use the party and battle displays to decide what the next turn needs.

The recent build includes repairs to target controls and battle interface connections. Real controller and headset testing remains part of the ongoing work.

STATUS EFFECTS

More Than Damage Numbers:

The combat system includes poison, turn-disrupting effects, accuracy and magic restrictions, and temporary stat changes. Their durations and effects give creators another way to shape encounters.

  • Poison: Damage over time that rewards planning ahead.
  • Stun, sleep and paralysis: Effects that can interrupt an opponent's actions.
  • Blind and silence: Pressure on attacks and ability use.
  • Buffs and debuffs: Temporary changes to combat strengths and weaknesses.

Start With Supported Effects:

The guided ability creator provides a small set of supported presets, including poison and a self-buff. More elaborate custom mechanics still need development and testing; they are not automatically created by the editor.
