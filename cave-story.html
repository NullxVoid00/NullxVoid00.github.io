Into the caves

A full adventure on the console.

The conversion follows the original freeware game, with added ways to share the journey.

01

Explore and uncover

Travel through connected areas, talk with the island’s inhabitants and follow the original quests and branching story.

02

Grow your arsenal

Collect weapons, manage ammunition and build weapon experience. The inventory, map and original weapon behaviors are part of the implemented game.

03

Bring a party

Up to four players can join the same adventure. Co-op keeps individual health and resources while sharing the world and its story progression.

Same island. More company.

A console adventure for the room.

Join from a controller

Insert the Cave Story disc, pick up a controller and press Jump / Space to connect. Choose Multiplayer on the title screen, select a character and ready up. People watching can stay spectators.

The conversion includes character selection and custom-character support, alongside shared room transitions and checkpoint recovery.

Save the adventure

Campaign save and checkpoint systems are implemented. Returning to the console Home screen clears held inputs so the game can be resumed cleanly.

Live reconnects, saves and complete campaign routes are still part of playtesting. Feature completion does not mean every possible session has been tested.
